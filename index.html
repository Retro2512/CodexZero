04 / Boundaries

What stays exactly the same.

01

Model and reasoning CodexZero does not reroute models or lower reasoning effort.

02

Prompts and permissions No prompt, tool, MCP, app, skill, plugin, or review capability is removed.

03

Installed Codex Signed Desktop packages and stock CLI binaries are not modified. Launcher uses Desktop’s supported CLI-path override.

04

Diagnostics Non-identical lines, exit codes, warnings, errors, and full raw artifacts remain available.
