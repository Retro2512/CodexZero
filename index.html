FAQ

Quick answers.

What CodexZero changes, what it keeps, and how the alternatives performed.

How is this different from RTK?

RTK rewrites the output of particular shell commands. CodexZero works inside its side-by-side Codex build and keeps the full result. In the repeated run, RTK scored 32/36 and used 18.70% more tokens than Codex; CodexZero Safe scored 29/36 and used 14.63% fewer.

How is this different from Headroom?

Headroom shortens more kinds of conversation history through an added service. CodexZero focuses on Codex command results and runs beside your existing install.

What about Caveman and Ponytail?

Caveman asks the model to speak more briefly. Ponytail asks it to write less code. CodexZero instead shortens repeated command results before the model rereads them.

Does the 84.0% fixture result predict session savings?

It is the result for eleven fixed command outputs. The complete benchmark charts above show what happened across whole tasks.

What changes in Max Savings?

It gives Codex a shorter built-in instruction sheet. Your project instructions, tools, model, and reasoning choice stay the same.

Do fewer input tokens mean lower cost or more requests?

They mean the model processed less input. In the repeated public run, that lined up with 7.40% lower API-equivalent cost. Your own result appears in codex-zero savings.
